## What this page does differently

- **Onboarding first.** One endpoint-name field and a NoSec/DTLS-PSK switch feed copy-ready commands for the Go demo client (`cmd/lwm2m-client`, one `go run` line, no hardware), Zephyr and nRF91 (NCS). The Zephyr and NCS flags were checked against the sample Kconfig and `prj.conf`; Zephyr's options are `CONFIG_NET_SAMPLE_LWM2M_SERVER`/`_ID`. Under the commands, a status line waits for that endpoint and links to it once it registers.
- **A list that answers the usual questions:** LwM2M version, transport and queue mode, security, last seen, and a countdown to lifetime expiry. On a phone it becomes cards.
- **A device view that shows values without clicking around.** It reads `/3/0` on open and shows manufacturer, model and firmware at the top. Objects, resources, types, units and operations come from the OMNA registry (core objects 0-28 plus 55 IPSO objects, embedded). Enumerations are taken from the registry descriptions (battery status, power source, network bearer) and IPSO sensor units from resource 5701. You can read an object, an instance or a resource, look at the raw response (JSON, content format and payload hex) and the registration payload, and see the last Send.
- **Live values.** One Observe per device path is shared by all visitors and streamed as server-sent events. Limits: 2 streams per visitor, 32 in total, 4 paths per device, 5 minutes per stream, at most 4 updates per second. It is cancelled on the device when the last viewer leaves.
- **Safe to leave open.** The page cannot write, execute, create, delete or start a firmware update. It never reads `/0` or `/21`, and reads are rate-limited. Strict CSP (`default-src 'none'`), no external requests, fonts self-hosted. About 17 kB of gzipped JS and CSS, no framework.
